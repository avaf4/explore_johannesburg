Food Guide: What to Try in JoBurg

Try Bunny Chow

This street favorite serves flavorful curry in a hollowed bread loaf and is perfect for first-time visitors.

More info about Bunny Chow

Fun Fact

Bunny chow originated in Durban but is now loved across South Africa, including JoBurg.

Find a Great Braai Spot

Look for local restaurants or weekend events where grilled meats and shared sides create a social dining vibe.

More info about Braai

Fun Fact

"Braai" is more than a meal; it is a social tradition centered around gathering and storytelling.

Explore Local Markets

Visit market districts for small plates, baked goods, and modern takes on traditional South African dishes.

More info about South African food markets

Fun Fact

Maboneng and nearby market hubs are popular for weekend food trails and live local music.
