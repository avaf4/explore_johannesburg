Culture Guide: First Impressions in JoBurg

Respect and Etiquette

Greet people first, stay open-minded, and be intentional about learning local customs in each neighborhood.

Fun Fact

Johannesburg is known for its multilingual identity, with English, Zulu, Sesotho, and Setwana often heard daily.

Vibrant City Life

From street art to live music and community markets, JoBurg blends local heritage with a modern creative scene.

Fun Fact

Sawubona means "I see you" in Zulu, reflecting a greeting style rooted in recognition and respect.

Community and Conversation

Take time to chat with locals at markets and cafes. Personal connection is a big part of the JoBurg experience.

Fun Fact

Code-switching between multiple languages in one conversation is common across Johannesburg.
