Top Attractions in Johannesburg

Constitution Hill

A former prison complex turned museum and court site that gives deep context to South Africa's democratic journey.

Fun Fact

Constitution Hill is home to South Africa's Constitutional Court, where public tours are available.

Maboneng + Mandela Square

Pair Maboneng's creative district with Nelson Mandela Square's iconic statue for a full day of culture and city energy.

Fun Fact

"Maboneng" means "place of light" in Sesotho, reflecting the neighborhood's arts-focused identity.

Apartheid Museum

This museum gives essential context for understanding modern Johannesburg through immersive historical exhibits.

Fun Fact

Visitors receive random entry tickets labeled by apartheid-era racial categories to frame the experience from the start.
