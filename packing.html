Packing Tips for Johannesburg

What to Bring

Pack breathable layers, good walking shoes, sunscreen, and a light jacket for cool evenings.

Fun Fact

Because of JoBurg's elevation, evenings can feel cooler than many visitors expect.

Travel Smart

Carry a portable charger, keep copies of key documents, and use a secure day bag for sightseeing.

Fun Fact

A reusable water bottle and daypack are must-haves for full-day city excursions.

Dress for Activities

Plan outfits that can shift from city walking tours to evening dinners without a full change.

Fun Fact

Neutral layers help you adapt quickly to JoBurg's changing day-to-night temperatures.
