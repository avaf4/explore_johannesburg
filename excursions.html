Best Excursions from Johannesburg

Soweto Day Tour

Explore local history and culture through museums, neighborhoods, and important landmarks tied to South Africa's story.

Fun Fact

Soweto is an acronym for South Western Townships and is home to rich cultural and political history.

Apartheid Museum Visit

Spend a reflective morning understanding the country's past and how that history shapes Johannesburg today.

Fun Fact

The museum is one of the most visited educational sites in South Africa for international travelers.

Cradle of Humankind

Take a half-day trip outside the city to discover caves, fossil history, and beautiful natural scenery.

Fun Fact

This UNESCO World Heritage Site contains some of the world's most significant early human fossil discoveries.
