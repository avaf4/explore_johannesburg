Discover Johannesburg, South Africa

Johannesburg is vibrant, multilingual, and full of energy. This guide helps first-time visitors from the U.S. get ready for local culture, smart packing, unforgettable excursions, must-try foods, and iconic sights.

Start Exploring

Local Phrase

"Welkom in Johannesburg" (Afrikaans) means "Welcome to Johannesburg."

Expect to hear many languages as you move through neighborhoods across the city.

Why JoBurg?

This city blends history, creativity, and modern life. From heritage sites to street markets and lively food scenes, Johannesburg offers something new around every corner.

Why Johannesburg Stands Out

Johannesburg is the heartbeat of modern South Africa, where rich history meets bold creativity. From iconic heritage landmarks to lively neighborhoods full of art, food, and music, the city offers first-time visitors an unforgettable blend of culture, energy, and discovery.
